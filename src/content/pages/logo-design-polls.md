We&#8217;ve created these logo polls in order to measure how professional brand and logo designers ( the main visitors of this site ) are feeling about some of the most famous and widely spread logo designs in the world. To make it more interesting we are setting against each other some competitive logos.

The results are valuable because they represent the opinion of logo and brand designers, people with visual culture, the same specialist that create the rest of the logos.

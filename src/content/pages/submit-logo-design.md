## 3 simple rules:

  1. Your logo/brand design story better be interesting :)
  2. Convince us with some text in English and attach the visual materials in good resolution ( up to 10 MB )
  3. Send everything as an e-mail to margarit/at/ralev.com with title &#8220;LOGO STORY&#8221;

Your story might be published here. Or not :) Make the first step and find out.

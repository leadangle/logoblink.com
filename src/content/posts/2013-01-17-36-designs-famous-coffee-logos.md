As the coffee is my favorite everyday drink I did a research on the coffee brands and their logos from around the world. I’ve collected the most interesting  of the famous coffee logos for you to see here!

Enjoy them:)

<img class="alignnone  wp-image-3509" title="Coffee brand logo designs pt. 1" src="/img/2013/01/coffee-logos-file-1.jpg" alt="world famous coffee logos" width="600" height="2724" />

It’s interesting how different the approach to each logo design is. The company’s logo is the most important part of the corporate identity. Therefore, brand logos should communicate with consumers and users on a personal level. In this way they are affecting the market’s opinions towards the brand on a psychological level.

The logos I have presented here serve to show how a good logo design can help for a company to become worldwide known and loved by the people.

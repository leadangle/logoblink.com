—

_“Top Symbols And Trademarks Of The World”_ was published in 1973, written by Franco Maria Ricci & Corinna Ferrari. There are two volumes of this Italian book, which are very hard to find now. Maybe if you’re lucky you’ll find them in some old bookshops. This rare pages are a part from the [Typogabor.com](http://www.typogabor.com/) collection. And we should all thank [Iancu](http://www.ibarbar.ro/blog/2008/03/28/top-symbols-and-trademarks-of-the-world/) for getting together all the pages into these 2 pdf files.

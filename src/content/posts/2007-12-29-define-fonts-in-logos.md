We all know and use the most popular fonts, but sometimes it’s hard to define fonts of a specific typeface, used in a logo, logotype or clear word-mark. Here comes a tool which is pretty useful. It’s called [“What The Font?!”](http://www.myfonts.com/WhatTheFont/) and it works like this:

1 – You upload / write a link to the logo image

2 – Click “Search”

3 – Check the suggestions of all the similar fonts that looks like the one on your image

If you are doing a research on the font of the SONY logo, for example, it will look like this:

Let’s present you one of the largest snowboard brands in the world – **[Burton](http://global.burton.com/).** It is founded by Jake Burton Carpenter in 1977 – he built the world’s first snowboard factory. His variety of products are marketed worldwide in over 4,340 stores with more than 1/3 being located in the US.

<img class="alignleft" title="Burton Snowboards logo" src="/img/2012/01/images-1.jpg" alt="arrow-like snowboards logo design" width="154" height="183" />The key to create a great logo design is flexibility, simplicity and adaptability. When using many details in a logo it becomes unreadable, overdone and difficult to adapt to brand materials. Simple logos are easy to recognize and remember. A well-designed logo is one that can be adapted to different sizes and be easily read by the viewer.

**Burton** created a clear, bold, iconic mark that would define the brand through the years. It represents a lower case “b”, a visual element related to the company’s name, and an upturned arrow. Because of the simplicity of the shape, the main aim is achieved. The Burton Snowboards logo design looks nice on each item it’s placed, no matter how small it is. When the arrow is scaled up to large size, it has even more impact.



**What is more interesting about Burton’s logo is that since 1977 the design has been changing every year. **

It’s colorful, it’s extravagant.. and it’s not the typical Euro Championship logo, but with the right presentation everything’s fine.

Actually the video is made back in 2009, but it’s a really good example of how a brand design should be approached and presented. If the [London 2012 Olympics logo](https://logoblink.com/london-2012-logo/) had the same presentation – I bet more people would be happy with the design. Anyway – this post is about the Euro 2012 football games, so I’ll provide some more images related to the topic:

Boom Festival is being held for the first time in 2008, Portugal. It is a complex event where a lot of performances meet. Watch this movie to meet the idea ..

`youtube: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rVCbPvEn8ro`



… and then watch the logo case, so that you can understand the idea of the symbol these guys came with :) 

`youtube: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GqK1hwSG0Fc`



I think the approach was great, but the final product could be better. For example – the logo will be used in a lot of mediums, so it has to be with stronger shapes that could be represented no matter the tools you are using. The thin lines could be a bit more “massive” …

I like the typo :) and the hole idea of trying to represent so many meanings in a symbol. It’s an effort that deserves to be seen. It will be great if you drop your comments bellow. I think this is an interesting topic to discuss…

RedBubble seller [satansbrand](http://www.redbubble.com/people/satansbrand/portfolio) mashed up the names of the houses from the popular _A Song of Fire and Ice_ fantasy saga with the logos of existing famous beer brands. The graphics are available as T-shirts, hoodies and stickers. The houses are easy to recognize due to the writing, but can you guess all beer brands?

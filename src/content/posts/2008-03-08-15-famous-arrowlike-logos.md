In my opinion these logos got arrows inside them or are at least arrow-shaped. Some may be called chevrons or something else, but still – these are arrowlike logos. This selection is made just for fun. I actually wanted to check how many famous logos include or resemble arrows I can find. [“Kinko’s”](http://www.kinkos.com/aa/) was one of the logos I added at the last moment. The truth is that the arrow symbols were well-hidden for me, despite my graphic design experience. :)

Another famous brand using an arrow element in the logo design is [Burton](https://logoblink.com/burton-snowboards-logo/).

I am sure there are a plenty other arrowlike logos which we use or see every day. Please share your observations with us in the comment box below.

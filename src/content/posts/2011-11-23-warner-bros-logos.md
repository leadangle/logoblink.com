After the 90’s the Warner Bros. Picture to keep the shield that characterizes them today, but continues with the need to “transform” it, by creating a chameleon image. With this new approach, they try to approach and address movie and create custom “shields”. In this way the logo is engaged with the character of the movie that Warner Bros. presents.

So this is the Rio 2016 Olympic logo. As every olympic logo – it’s in the center of attention for 90% of the graphic designers in the world… most oftenn accompanied by the words “I could design a better one” or “I can do this for 5 minutes”… etc. Yes, often a logo could be better designed or could be reproduced in 5 minutes. The thing is that a design is chosen for a reason and the faster you can memorize and reproduce it – the more successful it is probably going to be.

<span style="background-color: #ffff00;">I’ll try to be objective (as usual) – it’s more YES than NO. </span>

  * PROS: It’s visible in variety of scales, it’s recognizable, it has classic color scheme, it will do the work
  * CONS: It mixes 2D with 3D ( gradient mark with non-gradient logotype and olympic symbol ), although it’s recognizable it still reminds of many other logos.



<img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1344" title="examples of Olympic designs" src="/img/2011/01/olympic-community-logos.jpg" alt="olympic logo designs" width="600" height="273" />

This is a mini logo set of 3 other olympic logos, treating the problem in a similar way. The first one is a proposal that was used when China was a candidate for the Olympics. Later they changed the logo to the red, more “traditional” guy. You may ask yourself the question why they changed this not so bad working logo…  The answer for myself is that it’s not distinctive enough. Also because of the national propaganda :) The second one is the official Athens 2004 logo. A great one, although, personally I don’t like too illustrative logos. The Olympic wreath is updated and there are stylized people incorporated in the design. Pure classic. Well the background is a different story, but hey – they needed something non-designish. The third one is a Quenstown, New Zealand 2018 Winter Olympic Games [bid](http://www.splitmindsolutions.com/index.php?option=com_portfolio&id=34&view=item&Itemid=18). It’s also using the idea of the circle/elipse as a base.

Going back to the Rio 2016… and the really exploited topic about “people holding hands” ,”dancing people” , “people in a circle”…

<span style="background-color: #ffff00;">Why is this logo idea too exploited ?</span> Because of the internet boom during last 10 years and all the forums out there. Thousands of new online and offline communities are being created every day. How will you describe a forum with just one logo / sign ? The easiest way is to put several buddies in a circle. Dots are the heads and lines are the arms / bodies. Of course – every designer strives to do his best to upgrade the idea and leave a mark, but the problem is in the idea itself – it’s too overexposed. The logos below are real functioning logos, showing different approaches to the theme:

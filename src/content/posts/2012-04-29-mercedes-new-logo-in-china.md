Daimler, the world’s leading luxury car manufacturer, decided to create a joint venture with BYD, China’s leading economy car manufacturer and this is how **Denza** appeared. Basically – it will be the local “Mercedes”. That’s why I’ve used this catchy title.

The Denza brand is due to consist of a range of electric power vehicles with the first model set to use the underpinnings of the first generation Mercedes B-Class. This platform was chosen because its flat floor structure was designed to use batteries and therefore reduces the costs of development.
